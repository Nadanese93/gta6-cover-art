Neon Coast Cover Studio turns your own photos into GTA-style multi-panel cover art, fully offline in the browser. Pick 1–4 photos (or a sample), choose one of six layouts — full bleed, hero + three, four-panel, 2×2 grid, tilted snapshots, nine-panel — and one of five sunset/neon grades. Every panel photo runs through a real posterize + Sobel ink-outline + gradient-map + halftone pipeline, with per-panel drag-to-pan and zoom cropping, an Anton title with a sunset gradient, a Pacifico script subtitle, a star heat meter and a badge ribbon. Export the finished cover as a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080 or 1080×1920, with undo/redo throughout. No uploads, no accounts, no network — your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
